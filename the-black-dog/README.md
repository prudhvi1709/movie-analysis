# The Black Gold - Analysis

Data-backed release and promotion strategy for **The Black Gold**, Samyuktha Menon's pan-India crime thriller (a cop versus a coal-mafia syndicate, inspired by real events on the Telangana-Maharashtra border). Director Yogesh KMC, music Sam C S, producer Razesh Danda (Hasya Movies x Maganti Pictures).

## Key findings

- **Recommended release: Friday 7 August 2026.** Clear competitive gap after July's tentpoles, two back-to-back long weekends (Bonalu, then Independence Day) before Nani's *The Paradise* on 21 Aug. Alternative: 14 Aug. Avoid 3 to 24 July (Vishwambhara, Jailer 2).
- **Teaser 21 Jul, trailer 31 Jul**, both on high-engagement evening slots, timed around the competition.
- **Social reach is trade-driven:** one Taran Adarsh post (1 Jan 2026) drove ~73% of the film's visible reach on X across eight months. Brief the trade tier first.

## Method and limits

Built from public release calendars, festival/holiday data, and a live authenticated X pull (real view/like counts). It is decision-support, not a guarantee of outcome. Competitor dates shift, so re-verify before locking. Reddit was not read in full (reddit.com was blocked during research).
