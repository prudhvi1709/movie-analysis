SENTIMENT ANALYSIS DEEP DIVE

Why Spirit's 2.8% Negative Sentiment is Exceptionally Strong

THE CONTROVERSY = CURRENCY FORMULA

Animal's Launch Context (Pre-Teaser, 2022):
• Ranbir Kapoor + Sandeep Reddy Vanga = First collaboration announcement
• No controversy yet - People didn't know what to expect
• Sentiment: 31.7% positive, 65% NEUTRAL (most people had no opinion), 3.3% negative
• 65% neutral = Low energy, passive interest

SPIRIT'S LAUNCH CONTEXT (First Look, 2026):

After Animal's Massive Success + Controversy:
• Everyone NOW knows Sandeep Reddy Vanga's style (polarizing, intense)
• ACTUAL Sentiment: 40.6% positive, 56.5% neutral, 2.8% NEGATIVE
• Only 2.8% negative = Exceptionally positive reception
• Very few critics - minimal backlash compared to Animal's launch

WHY 2.8% NEGATIVE IS EXCEPTIONAL:

1. Overwhelmingly Positive Reception

• Animal's pre-teaser: 3.3% negative (similar to Spirit)
• Spirit: 2.8% negative = Even lower than Animal
• Minimal backlash despite high expectations post-Animal success

2. Strong Fan Enthusiasm

• 40.6% positive = High excitement and anticipation
• Only 15 negative tweets out of 529 analyzed
• Indicates genuine fan excitement without major controversy

3. Neutral as "Wait & See"

• 56.5% neutral = Curious but not yet committed
• These are potential converters after trailer/release
• Clean slate - not turned off by controversy • Animal's critics amplified its cultural impact
