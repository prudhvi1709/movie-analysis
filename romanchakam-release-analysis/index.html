The Jathi Ratnalu comparison cuts both ways

Sometimes praise ("full Jathi Ratnalu vibe, full ga navvi"), sometimes the sharpest insult ("don't expect another Jathi Ratnalu," 78-score Reddit comment). The film is being graded against that specific bar, not a neutral one.

What this means for the team

  • Lead Week 2 marketing with comedy and music, not romance. Every channel agrees on both calls.
  • Get ahead of the "imitation" comparison directly before it hardens into the film's defining criticism.
  • Stop templated "review" posts. They feed the "paid review" backlash visible on both platforms.
  • Push the Mahesh Babu endorsement harder than any other asset - it outperforms every organic post by an order of magnitude.
  • Dig into the Andhra Pradesh gap. Promotion, screen count, or genuine reception - three different fixes.
